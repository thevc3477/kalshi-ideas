Partnership activation ideas

Built on deals Kalshi already has.

These are my proposals, not existing Kalshi plans. Each one starts from a public partnership and is meant to turn those rights into something fans can walk into or come back for.

01 · Chicago · Blackhawks

Fans vs. the Market

A night at the United Center with live Kalshi odds next to a live fan poll. Every time they split, the crowd picks a side. I already built the comparison layer in Fan Pulse.

Builds onThe Chicago Blackhawks partnership, announced December 23, 2025, including signage at the United Center.
02 · Music · Culture

A room for music

The House of GOAL format, applied to music: live market screens on the next awards show or festival headliner, and a room full of fans arguing with the odds.

Builds onKalshi's House of GOAL activation in Brooklyn, July 2026, which put a hero bar and live market screens in the room.
03 · New York · Madison Square Garden

Concert nights at the Kalshi Concourse

On concert nights at the Garden, put culture markets on the screens and let the crowd vote against them between sets.

Builds onThe Madison Square Garden partnership, announced May 2026, which named the sixth-floor Kalshi Concourse and includes visibility at concerts and comedy.
04 · Creators · Weekly

Crowd vs. Odds

A weekly creator segment that picks the game where fans and the market disagree most, argues both sides, then checks back after the final whistle. The split can be pulled from Fan Pulse.

Builds onKalshi's creator and talent work, including its first athlete partnership, and Fan Pulse.
05 · Chicago · Album release

Record store crawl

A Chicago record store crawl for an album release. Fans predict first-week numbers, and every stop shows the live market. I'd run it through the record store network I already work with.

Builds onCulture markets already live through the PrizePicks partnership, and the Chicago record store network I built with Vinyl Collectiv.
